What I Did

Answered customer enquiries

Handled a high volume of calls about accounts, cards, transfers and banking products.

Verified and protected

Confirmed the caller's identity before sharing any account details, following the bank's security rules.

Solved complaints

Listened to the problem, found the cause and solved it on the call, or escalated it to the right department and followed up.

Explained products

Explained banking products and fees in simple words so customers could make the right choice.

Kept accurate records

Logged every call and case clearly so the next agent or team had the full picture.

I kept consistently high customer satisfaction ratings by staying calm, being clear, and owning each case until it was closed.

Supporting New Joiners

Chosen by my team lead

Helping new agents through their first weeks

My team lead nominated me to support new agents during their first weeks at NBE. I sat side by side with them on live calls so they could see how real cases are handled, and then I listened while they took calls and helped them when they needed it.

Being chosen for this showed that my team lead trusted both my work quality and the way I deal with people.

  • Side-by-side callsNew joiners listened to my calls to learn the flow, the tone and the bank's procedures.
  • Live supportI stayed with them on their first calls and helped with difficult questions.
  • Systems and proceduresI showed them how to use the systems, check customer details and escalate cases correctly.
  • Feedback and confidenceI gave simple tips after calls so they could handle customers confidently on their own sooner.
