My Projects

Two areas I owned at Almosafer. Click a card to see the details.

Project 1 · Abuse Tracking

I analysed every transaction that happened in the company, online and offline, on a dataset of more than 1 million rows. For offline bookings I reviewed activity per agent. The goal was to find anything against company rules and processes, and any bug in the website or app that could be used to take money or rewards the wrong way.

1 · CollectAll online and offline booking transactions, 1M+ rows, using SQL and Python.
2 · DetectRun my checks to find patterns against company rules, per agent and per channel.
3 · Add new checksEvery time a new type of abuse appeared, I added a new check so it is caught next time.
4 · InvestigateConfirm the case with the data before any action is taken.
5 · ActSend each case to the right team to recover the money or close the gap.
Offline bookings

Actions against company rules

When I found an agent action against company rules, I prepared the evidence from the data and aligned with the Legal team to recover the lost money for the company.
Online bookings

Gaps and bugs in the website or app

When I found a gap in the website or app, I documented how it happened and sent it to the internal tech team to close it or fix it.

I also built Tableau dashboards that tracked abuse trends and risk KPIs. The Risk and Operations teams used them every day, and I sent weekly insight reports to senior management.

Project 2 · Loyalty Programme Protection

I had full access to the company's loyalty programme and to every client's wallet, and I was responsible for making sure all wallets were correct. That meant checking that points were earned and used the right way, catching abuse cases, and dealing with them quickly.

Add pointsFix missing points so the client gets what they earned.
Remove pointsTake back points given by mistake or through abuse.
