My Role and Goal

I own the data and reporting for multi-market digital campaigns. I build the live dashboards, explain the results to clients in weekly reviews, and recommend where the budget should go next.

My goal is simple: make sure every campaign achieves the targets in its media plan, on budget and on time. When the objective is purchases, my focus is to get the highest possible ROAS, so the client earns more revenue for every dollar spent.

AwarenessReach and impressionsDeliver the planned reach at or below the planned CPM.
TrafficClicks and quality visitsHit the click target at or below the planned CPC, with a healthy CTR.
Leads and messagesLeads at the right costReach the lead target while keeping cost per lead within plan.
PurchasesPurchases and high ROASGrow sales and revenue while keeping cost per purchase low.
ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad spend

A ROAS of 4.0x means the campaign brought $4 in revenue for every $1 spent on ads.

Built with Claude

Campaign Pacing Check: a daily AI health check for every campaign

I combined my analytics work with AI by building a Claude skill called campaign-pacing-check. Every morning it compares the live Looker Studio report with the media plan, checks whether each KPI is keeping up with the money spent, finds the platform or objective that is underperforming, and sends the digital team a short email. This means problems are caught early, before the end of the campaign.

1 · Media planReads the planned targets and budget for each objective and platform from Google Drive.
2 · Live reportOpens the Looker Studio report and reads spend, impressions and clicks.
3 · Pacing checkCalculates spend pacing vs KPI pacing in Python, not by hand.
4 · Platform breakdownFor any KPI that is behind, filters each platform to find where the gap comes from.
5 · Daily emailSends "On track" or "RED FLAG" with clear tables to the digital team through Gmail.
The rule it follows
Spend pacing = spend ÷ planned budget  ·  KPI pacing = actual result ÷ media plan target
If the KPI pacing is 5 points or more behind the spend pacing, it is flagged as BEHIND. Example: 70% of the budget is spent but only 60% of the impressions target is delivered, so the gap is 10 points and it is flagged.

Built with Claude

From live report to a finished client summary

At the end of every campaign the client needs a clear written summary. I built a second Claude skill, campaign-eoc-summary, that reads the Looker Studio report and the media plan, analyses each objective using the cost-per-metric view, writes the summary in plain English, and then fills it into the report's Summary page after I approve it.

1 · Media planReads every tab, including additional budget, and records the target metric, target and planned cost for each line.
2 · OverviewRecords every KPI vs target and total spend vs budget.
3 · BreakdownFilters one objective at a time and ranks platforms by actual cost per metric.
4 · Placements and languageFinds the top placements and the best creative language for each objective.
5 · DemographicsFinds the best age range and gender for each objective.
6 · Write and fillWrites the summary, I review it, then it is filled into the report's Summary page.
Rules that keep it accurate
  • The media plan decides which metric belongs to each objective. It never guesses.
  • Best platform = lowest actual cost per metric. Weakest platform = highest actual cost per metric.
  • Every number comes from the report or the media plan, and every calculation is checked (CPM, CPC, cost per result).
  • Nothing is published before I approve the draft.
